== Purpose

Focus on one Operational Capability and the participants involved in it.
It answers the question: Who is involved in achieving this operational capability?

== Position in Arcadia

Operational Analysis.

== Main concepts

* *Operational Capability*: The operational goal selected for examination.
* *Operational Entity or Actor*: A participant involved in the capability.
* *Involvement*: The relationship between the capability and a participant.
* *Capability relationship*: A relationship linking the capability to another capability.
