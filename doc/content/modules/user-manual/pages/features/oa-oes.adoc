== Purpose

Describe an operational scenario through the interactions of entities, actors, or roles.
It answers the question: How do operational participants interact during a particular situation?

== Position in Arcadia

Operational Analysis.

== Main concepts

* *Participant lifeline*: An operational entity, actor, or role taking part in the scenario.
* *Interaction message*: An interaction exchanged between the participants.
* *Message ordering*: The order of the interactions within the scenario.
