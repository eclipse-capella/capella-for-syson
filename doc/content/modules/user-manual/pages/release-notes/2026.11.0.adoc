= 2026.11.0
:page-audience: 🧭 Systems engineers, ⚙️ DevOps, and 💻 developers

== Key highlights

* Two Operational Analysis diagrams are now available: the xref:user-manual:features/oabd.adoc[OABD] and the xref:user-manual:features/oaib.adoc[OAIB].
* Diagram filters are available in the OAB and the LAB.
* Realization relationships between engineering levels can be edited in the Details view and navigated in the Semantic Browser.

== New features

* In Operational Analysis:

** The xref:user-manual:features/oabd.adoc[OABD — Operational Activity Breakdown] displays the decomposition of Operational Activities, with requirements and their Describes links.
+
image::oabd-ife.png[OABD showing the breakdown of the operational activities of the In-Flight Entertainment System sample model,width=75%]
** The xref:user-manual:features/oaib.adoc[OAIB — Operational Activity Interaction Blank] describes the interactions between Operational Activities.
Activities and requirements can be created in the diagram or dropped from the explorer.
+
image::oaib-ife.png[OAIB showing the interactions between operational activities of the In-Flight Entertainment System sample model,width=75%]
** Operational Activities can be created and dropped in the xref:user-manual:features/oab.adoc[OAB].

* In diagrams:

** The *Show Activities* filter of the OAB and the *Show Functions* filter of the LAB control the display of the allocated activities and functions.
+
image::lab-ife.png[LAB with the Diagrams Options menu open on the Show Functions filter,width=75%]

* In the Details view and the Semantic Browser:

** The *Capella* page of the Details view displays the elements realized by the selected element and, read-only, the elements realizing it.
+
image::capella-properties-ife.png[Details view showing the Realizing System Functions of the Watch Movie operational activity,width=75%]
** The Semantic Browser displays the same realization relationships.
See xref:user-manual:features/editor.adoc[the project editor].

* In the application:

** The {product} version is displayed in the application footer.
** Dialogs that select a model element display the hierarchy of the Capella explorer.

== Bug fixes

* In diagrams:

** In the OCB, a Generalization connects two distinct capabilities, and an Involvement connects a capability to an Operational Entity or an Operational Actor.
** Operational Interactions targeting an ancestor activity are rejected, at creation and at reconnection.
** Requirement Describes links have a uniform appearance in all diagrams.
** The *New Describes* and *Link to Element* actions are available again in diagrams.
** Colors, icons, and border sizes are more consistent in the Operational Analysis diagrams, the SAB, and the LAB.
** The Functional Context Diagram can be displayed again in the *Dynamic Contextual View* panel.

* In the explorer:

** Operational Activity ports are always hidden, including when the *Hide Ports* filter is disabled.
** Operational Entities, Operational Capabilities, and requirements display their icons.

* In SysON projects:

** The SysON onboarding is available again for projects that are not Capella projects.

== Improvements

* In diagrams:

** Changing the direction of a port updates the direction of the opposite port.
** Actors and human actors have a consistent appearance in all perspectives.
** The SAB has an improved style.
** Element creation is faster, especially when many elements are created.

* In the application:

** Capella type checks and queries are faster on large models.
