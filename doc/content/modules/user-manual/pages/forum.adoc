= Community

The https://mbse-capella.org/capella-for-syson.html[official project website] presents the initiative and provides a contact for getting involved.

== Share an Arcadia use case

Explain the engineering activity, the modeling result you need, and the difficulty you encounter.
Use examples from the relevant Arcadia perspective so that the project team can understand the engineering context.

== Follow product feedback

Use the {source-code-url}/issues[issue tracker] to find reported problems and enhancement requests.
See xref:contribute.adoc[] to prepare a report or proposal.
For assistance with your installation, see xref:support.adoc[].
